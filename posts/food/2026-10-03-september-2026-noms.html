october 2026

this is technically september noms with the first two days of october smuggled in because i was not going to split the very end of my la trip into another post (╥ ᴗ ╥)

there was so much good food this month . . including a new favourite markham sushi place , a trader joe's tasting tour , multiple la milkshake comparisons and one surprisingly excellent meal from a gas station ꉂ(˵˃ ᗜ ˂˵) not everything has pictures but they were all worth mentioning ! even so 46 pictures is a lot , enjoy going through them ~

before la :

  • – 大碗山西刀削面 / knife cut noodles : meatballs , sesame / peanut dry noodles and popcorn chicken ! everything was good , although i still think the dry noodles from hey noodle were better . . clearly i need to get them again for a proper scientific comparison
  • – a paris baguette assortment : an iced matcha latte , strawberry & crème mini croissant , blueberry cake with strawberry filling , hash brown / cheese / ham bread and custard choux . the “ grilled cheese ” arrived very not grilled and wasn't good until i put it under the broiler myself , at which point it became an actual melty grilled cheese and was much better. i also eat everything with chopsticks , including desserts ₍ᐢ•ᴗ•ᐢ₎♡
  • – sushi kombu's all salmon set and kombu maki set ! ! salmon sashimi , salmon nigiri , spicy salmon , salmon avocado rolls and a huntsville roll with spicy scallop , avocado , cucumber and salmon . this is officially my favourite sushi place of all time now : huge portions , excellent value and an insane filling-to-rice ratio ૮₍ ˃ ⤙ ˂ ₎ა
  • – when the owners of sushi kombu went on vacation i had to do some sushi comparisons : j san sushi & market was good but had noticeably smaller portions , while makirrito's salmon lover plate was much closer to what i wanted . sushi kombu still wins , followed by makirrito and then j san
  • – a matcha drink from machi machi that came in a special . . plastic bottle . . . would not recommend , why would i pay extra for a drink smaller than the regular cups . . fell for marketing tactics , aesthetic not worth the cost
  • – minced pork noodles and taiwanese popcorn chicken from ten ren's before / after a nail appointment . always solid and extremely convenient

while staying in la :

  • – the stand's ABC burger and strawberry milkshake , which were both extremely good . bf got the havarti shroom burger the first time and a cheeseburger with fried egg + chili when we returned . i also tried their macaroni and cheese on visit two ! somehow i took absolutely no photos despite this being one of my fav places we ate at . too yummy and ate them too quickly i guess , , . . two burgers and two shakes costing over $40 usd after tax and tip was ridiculous though
  • – AYCE hot pot at shabuya in alhambra : my picks were all the meats , korean triangle fish cakes , wood ear mushrooms , bok choy , quail eggs , noodles and chicken karaage . i discovered that stacking multiple sauces in one tiny cup does not mean they will mix merely because i stir them . . i just created distinct sauce layers (ᵕ—ᴗ—) we went to handel's afterwards for a waffle boat with banana cream pie and strawberry ice cream , so any hot-pot dessert i may have missed was irrelevant ( note that the ice cream picture i took was over half eaten and of the day after lol )
  • – wingstop boneless wings , we got a lot of flavours but my favs were garlic parmesan , lemon pepper and mild ! they also have good seasoning on the fries ⸜(｡˃ ᵕ ˂)⸝♡ the ranch was solid but the blue cheese could have been stronger
  • – evidently we went on a snack run to trader joe's : mac & cheese , strawberry mochi gummies , dark chocolate crispy rice sticks , chocolate chip waffles , pineapple juice & triple chip crispy cookies ( i prefer tiny crispy crunchy chocolate chip cookies in the large tub but we didn't get those ) excellent , no notes ~ satisfied my tastes for sure
  • – trader joe's gone berry crazy dark-chocolate-covered strawberry pieces were another yummy treat ! i actually preferred them after they thawed a little because the strawberry became softer and less icy . trader joe's frozen japchae was the opposite though , far too many onions and i could not finish it (╥﹏╥)
  • – sunny's sushi hut in north hollywood looked a little sketch from outside but was completely normal , just small :3 i liked my spicy salmon salad and alaska rolls , although bf was less impressed by his tempura and chicken cutlet . probably wouldn't get again even if i personally would have ordered it for sushi alone
  • – sushi goken was the next experiment and a much better staple candidate ! the sashimi roll had tuna , salmon , albacore , imitation crab , avocado , tobiko and salmon roe , while the salmon fusion roll had spicy tuna , cucumber , fresh salmon , microgreens and yuzu ponzu . i failed to read closely enough to realize the latter was spicy but it was surprisingly good anyway
  • – i have also started genuinely enjoying the little salads that come with japanese meals because goma / sesame dressing is really really good . i may get a large bottle for home lol
  • – kluckin' chicken bowls were not really for me because i do not like american pickles or coleslaw , but the chicken and spicy sauce were good . the vanilla milkshake with oreo topping was easily my highlight and became another entry in the accidental milkshake ranking system
  • – frozen ikea meatballs with leftover kluckin' sauce and trader joe's mac & cheese . certified yum , good combo (˶>⩊<˶)
  • – hook burger's crispy chicken sandwich with fries and ranch was very good , plus there was a random orange on the side that was unexpectedly sweet and good
  • – the hat's pastrami dip was divine ( i cant stop thinking about it ) and the chili cheese fries were also alright ( a little bland . . ) apparently eating the sandwich and fries separately rather than combining them makes me a heretic , but i stand by it ! i also relearned that american “ iced tea ” can literally mean cold unsweetened tea . canadian iced tea instincts betrayed me
  • – mango boba from chicha san chen , kindly acquired by bf's mom and sister ! very cute presentation and pretty good , although i don't drink boba that frequently & am not enough of a tapioca fan to provide other analysis
  • – chicken katsu at katsujin with bf and his family . the little sesame-sauce ritual was very fun : grind the goma yourself with a wooden pestle and then add the katsu sauce over it ~ the set also came with agedashi tofu , pickled vegetables , cabbage , rice and miso soup . extremely busy with a long wait and no online waitlist , but still completely worth visiting
  • – bread & breakfast's pastrami bowl with eggs , tater tots and extra cheese . the mild cilantro lime crema unexpectedly tasted more like the salsa verde from tops , while the chunkier serrano-based house salsa looked correct but was WAY too spicy for both of us . i wanted a perfect mixture of the two but was too afraid of ruining the entire bowl
  • – finally visited the hello kitty and friends cafe at universal citywalk ! bf and i expected very cute themed food that might be mediocre in actual flavour , but everything was considerably better than expected ⸜(｡˃ ᵕ ˂)⸝♡
  • – the chococat anniversary milk tea had chocolate milk tea , brown sugar boba and a tremendous amount of chocolate whipped topping . the berry & cream churros were berry-stuffed with cream-cheese frosting , and we bought three because they were buy two get one free . the strawberry bundt cake had strawberry vanilla-bean cake , jam , glaze and cream-cheese frosting . obviously very cute , but also genuinely tasty rather than relying on appearance to fool people
  • – krispy krunchy chicken after universal : chicken tenders , enormous potato wedges and biscuits from a location inside a gas station near bf's apartment . the chicken was shockingly good for what was essentially gas-station food . . well seasoned , crunchy and good value imo (˶ᵔ ᵕ ᵔ˶) i would eat it again but would prefer to acquire it somewhere that does not require lingering inside a gas station
  • – california fish grill : a caesar avocado salad with atlantic salmon for me , battered fish and fries for bf , plus clam chowder to share . solid and dependable
  • – porto's ham , cheese , lettuce and mayo croissant sandwiches while picking up food for bf's family . all without tomato but i think tomatoes would have been nice to keep :<
  • – white chocolate raspberry mini cake from nothing bundt cakes for bf's dad's birthday ! we all got our own choices of flavour and raspberry + white chocolate cake with cream-cheese frosting was mine ~ definitely sweet but not in a bad way :D
  • – final restaurant outing was el torito happy hour with bf's family . i tried the mango margarita from their margarita flight , and the tajín rim caught me off guard ! given my extremely low alcohol tolerance , having bbq chicken quesadillas , crispy flautas , queso dip and freshly made chips alongside it was definitely necessary
  • – the flautas were one of my favourites even though i preferred them without the sauces they came with . possibly strange , except bf's mom agreed with me so i have support. overall it was a very yummy mexican-food experience for someone who does not get much exposure to mexican cuisine at home in markham

a very successful food month . . i miss it already ( •̯́ ₃ •̯̀)
