october 2026 noms

november 2026

october was a great month for noms (˶ˆ꒳ˆ˵) sushi kombu has become the standard that every other sushi order now has to compare to , although a couple of small restaurants i visited were some of the nicest discoveries this month ! ᕙ( •̀ ᗜ •́ )ᕗ

  • – the keg : went with a friend and started with the complimentary bread and very fluffy whipped butter , which were good as expected . the broccoli gratin was more like broccoli covered in a good macaroni-and-cheese-style sauce than what i had pictured , and could have used a stronger flavour . not bad , just a little bland and different from my expectations . the tuna tartare was much more exciting : avocado underneath , a crispy cracker alongside and lime to squeeze over everything . i had tried it years ago and wanted to know if it was still as good as i remembered . . thankfully yes ! i would get it more often if it were less expensive (╥﹏╥)
  • – my friend's keg order was the 18 oz prime rib with fried onions , horseradish , au jus and garlic mashed potatoes , which he highly recommends ! dessert was billy miner pie and crème brûlée , i particularly liked the pie's chocolate crust because it reminded me of a brownie ~ my crème brûlée was served warm and i really enjoyed it too . my friend was surprised because he was used to the little cold ones at buffets lol
  • – sushi-q at markville mall : salmon avocado rolls and salmon nigiri for me , spicy salmon rolls for my friend . pretty good overall , although i still prefer sushi kombu for the price and quality . i showed her the pictures from last month's order and the difference in the filling / fish-to-rice ratio was very apparent . .
  • – CHICHA san chen's HEARTSTEEL collaboration : went at my friend's request and discovered that the chain is also in canada , which was a funny coincidence after trying it for the first time in la the previous month ! we got the honey cassia black tea and each received a sett photocard ~ the tea tasted good , but we were not convinced it justified the collaboration price . presumably the collectible accounts for some of that . . unfortunately both the drink and sett together still did not convince me it was worthwhile ( ;´ - `;)
  • – 3A noodle 三品麵食 : took my grandparents to this very small restaurant and was extremely happy with the portions for the price ! i got beef brisket and tendon soup with rice noodles , while they had beef brisket dry noodles that also came with some tendon as a welcome surprise . i tried the house XO sauce after seeing people mention it in reviews : dried scallop , dried shrimp and ham , with a thick almost jam-like consistency . delicious , but i added too much and definitely felt it in my tongue and throat . . my grandparents enjoyed the food a lot and were glad i found the place and took them there , which made the visit even better ♡
  • – swiss chalet for a pseudo-thanksgiving family lunch ! i skipped the seasonal meal because i do not particularly like the traditional fixings and got a quarter chicken , dark meat , with poutine on the side . it tasted about the same as it has for years , which is perfectly fine with me . there was also pumpkin pie , but it was served cold and i much prefer it warm . pumpkin pie is already not one of my favourite desserts , so the temperature did not help its case (｡•́︿•̀｡)
  • – T&T supermarket : salmon crazy sushi set , a salmon avocado roll and a pineapple sunset drink . the sushi was not bad at all for grocery-store sushi ! portions were not particularly generous , but it is a convenient option in a pinch .
  • – GB hand-pulled noodles during the downtown trip to visit the EB games nintendo flagship with a friend . i got beef brisket soup noodles and chose extra-thin noodles because i love having that option (˶ᵔ ᵕ ᵔ˶) the noodles were really good and the portion was good for the price , although the bowl leaned more toward noodles than the extremely meat-generous one at 3A . the matcha slushie was considerably less yum : weak matcha , almost vanilla-like flavour and a very icy texture . i kept thinking of the difference between freezer-burned ice cream and smooth soft serve . would return for the noodles , would not get the matcha again
  • – sushi kiku : a sushi place i tried because i got discount offers . got the salmon lover set and a salmon avocado roll , which were pretty nice overall ! worth considering for what i paid , but i would not buy the same order at full price .
  • – my grandfather's birthday dinner was an assortment of takeout and home-cooked dishes : spicy battered fish , stir-fried beef with noodles , crispy pork belly , char siu , lamb braised with carrots and garlic broccoli . everything was very enjoyable and i got to take leftovers home too ♡
  • – shodo sushi was another experiment : salmon lover's plate , chicken karaage and an empress roll . actually really good ! i particularly liked their very saucy take on karaage , and the presentation was attractive too . the packaging felt much larger than necessary for the food inside though , and i had difficulty justifying the portions at an undiscounted price . the food is a lot better than the idea of paying full price for it ( ;´ - `;)
  • – returned to sushi kombu for the torched set with torched salmon nigiri and boston rolls . . really good , unsurprisingly ! i have noticed that torched salmon sushi feels more filling to me than raw salmon sushi . i wonder if the richness has something to do with it ? regardless , delicious (˶ˆᗜˆ˵)
  • – bear bear cafe & kitchen with my grandparents ! a tiny little 茶餐廳 / hong kong-style café , with what felt like enough room for about three tables . they shared a teriyaki chicken rice bowl and i got the taiwanese popcorn chicken rice bowl , which was simple , filling and enjoyable . good portions for the price and very nice service too ! the main menu during our visit was only in chinese , with separate time-based breakfast / afternoon tea offerings , so translation was needed for reading the menu . definitely somewhere worth visiting again ♡
