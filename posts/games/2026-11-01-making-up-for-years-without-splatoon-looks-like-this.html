making up for years without splatoon looks like this

november 2026

splatoon got squeezed into the end of last month's games post , which felt unfair then & now considering it is 100% my favourite nintendo franchise to date . . i am pretty obsessed with it , and it's obvious from the shrine ” (˶ˆᗜˆ˵)

i have been thinking about why splatoon 3 clicked so quickly when competitive games are not usually my first choice . i get a lot of satisfaction from controlling space and helping with the objectives . there are ways to feel useful even when i am having a less successful time directly fighting people , and the movement and weapons are fun enough that i actually want to get better at using them . the visual style , characters and universe have also made it very easy to become attached ꒰ᐢ. ̫ .ᐢ꒱

the bloblobber is my main and default answer to most situations . i love the bouncing projectiles and being able to put pressure on an area , and it feels much more comfortable to me than the standard sloshers . however , i have started trying shooters too ! the aerospray RG & neo splash-o-matic have both been enjoyable , which is interesting considering how little interest i initially had in leaving my bubble bath ever . other weapons are still more successful for me in pve than pvp ( ;´ - `;)

splat zones remains my favourite anarchy mode because having a clear area to control suits how i like to play . tower control has grown into second place , and i have even come around to clam blitz ! the bloblobber has not felt like a particularly good fit for me there though , so getting more comfortable taking a shooter into that mode is something to work toward . overall i prefer anarchy to turf war , especially when zones is available ~

salmon run is a fun pve option , although mr grizz's weapon assignments can be a very efficient way of reminding me what i am bad at . . egg throwing and delivery give me trouble sometimes (╥﹏╥) splatoon raiders has been very enjoyable alongside it : the weapons and progression feel satisfying , and i like having that different pve experience alongside salmon run's loadouts and chaotic shifts . between the two games & splatoon 3 dlc , there's lots of great pve to experience :3

communication errors were making matches frustrating enough that i eventually connected the switch 2 dock by ethernet , which helped me feel much more confident about the connection . separately , a ranked update : i have made it from C+ to A+ ! ᕙ( •̀ ᗜ •́ )ᕗ

outside of the games themselves : my splatoon shrine is live ! anarchy snapshots , smallfry pictures , plaza photos and an amiibo cabinet ♡ i wanted somewhere for all the things i enjoy about the franchise to live together , and it has become a very fun corner of this site to work on .

my physical amiibo collection has been built too , and all 25 figures i currently own are finally set up across two display cases ! one groups the splatoon 1 / 2 figures and the other has splatoon 3 / raiders . seeing them together after tracking them down makes me so happy (⸝⸝ᵕᴗᵕ⸝⸝) the collection is still missing the purple inkling boy , orange inkling squid , and original pearl and marina . i have left room for them in the existing cases , so hopefully november will be kind to my search ~

elsewhere in october gaming , bf and i had a cobblemon resurgence earlier in the month before moving on to a new modded minecraft run with minecolonies , tinkers' construct and other mods from his curated favourites . minecraft remains very good at getting me to start another world . . apparently a fresh combination of familiar mods is enough to make doing that appealing all over again . it has been nice having something to play together alongside all the time i have been spending on the switch ♡

and a small follow-up to last month's switch lite update : my old friend has inherited it ! i am happy it has found another home after my upgrade (˶ᵔ ᵕ ᵔ˶)
