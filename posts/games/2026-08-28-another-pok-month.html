another pokémonth

september 2026

after watching the internet express devastation at the nintendo announcement that pokémon bank will be shutting down in february 2027 , i took it upon myself to really sit down and figure out what pokémon my bf needed moved from his old cartridges to home ᕙ( •̀ ᗜ •́ )ᕗ

we took a few hours one august evening to go through them all , letting the cartridge memory get refreshed in the 3ds with each check too as a bonus (⸝⸝ᵕᴗᵕ⸝⸝) i also took the opportunity to make backups of all of bf's saves in each game , so if a time came where the cartridges corrupted beyond use anymore they would still be there to play on a new one !

tl;dr - pokémon black is the main piece of the puzzle that i will need to play through in order to transport about 20 boxes of pokémon from heartgold to home . . because gen 4 games do not directly connect to poké transporter ( and therefore also not bank ) , i have to unlock the national dex in black ( not complete it , only unlock its accessibility ! ! ) to enable the ds link transfer from heartgold to black . and then once all the pokémon are in pokémon black i can send them to the poké transporter , and then pokémon bank , and then finally pokémon home (つ╥﹏╥)つ

and unlocking the national dex in pokémon black is essentially playing through the main story and defeating ( there are no spoilers for a 16 year old game , right ? ) N and Ghetsis . luckily for me bf's save was already halfway through , with five gym badges collected ( ‘• ω • `) but as much as i would probably enjoy playing through every side mission and non main part of black , i just don't have the time ( or willingness ) to drag out this playthrough over months . .

so i'm opting to collect mystery gifts using an old dns that still has them available ( who wouldn't want a bunch of super cool and otherwise unobtainable pokémon ? they're cool pieces of history from the game's release period ) to help me get through the game - so far a ray rizzo's shiny metagross and abram's cloyster from vcg 2013 have been doing a lot of heavy lifting , but i'm happy to have added other cool pokémon to bf's collection such as shiny palkia , dialga & giratina from summer 2013 (˶ˆ꒳ˆ˵)

my experience with pokémon black so far has been really good , except for finding out first hand how annoying constant random encounters every few steps in the old games were ( pre gen 7 or 8 ? ) . . . i have armed myself with 20 max repels that i dont think will last me all that long ( ;´ - `;) i also feel a bit silly for not having the type strengths and weaknesses memorised but oh well , i'll get to practice remembering them as i play ꒰ᐢ. ̫ .ᐢ꒱
