october 2026

i have finally used the UNLEASHIA Satin Wear Healthy-Green Cushion in n22 enough times to report back after last month's entire shade matching investigation . . and the good news is that i actually really like it ! (˶ˆᗜˆ˵)

it is very buildable , but more importantly it can stay sheer and natural enough to even out my skin without making my face look completely flat . with heavier foundation i've usually felt like i have to add all of the dimension back afterward , but with this i can put on blush and feel done . contour is optional ! this is perhaps a normal observation for other people but a major development for me ૮ ˶ᵔ ᵕ ᵔ˶ ა

the less good news is that my original understanding of how much product i needed was very incorrect . two full presses of the side pump is already too much for my entire face . . i initially assumed one press was approximately cheeks-and-nose quantity , only for the second press to cover my forehead , chin , jaw and neck with plenty of enthusiasm ( ;´ - `;) partial presses are very much the answer here : start with less , tap instead of drag , and only add more where i actually need it .

i also made the extremely logical decision that if i had accidentally applied a heavier base , i should make every other part of my makeup heavier too so that it would look balanced . this did technically make everything match . . it also made the entire result heavier than i like (˘･_･˘) the actual solution was not to escalate the situation , but to use less cushion in the first place . . the product is at its best for me when i let it be the light , easy base i bought it to be instead of trying to use it like a full coverage foundation .

otherwise it has been very forgiving ! i try to tap rather than drag the puff around , and it generally evens itself out well enough even though i have never really been a base applying person . my tarte amazonian clay blush layers nicely over it without needing setting spray between every step , and one final round of setting spray keeps everything in place (∩˃o˂∩)♡

the cushion does have spf 30 , but cushion application is absolutely not a substitute for applying enough dedicated sunscreen ( even if i have occasionally behaved like it is . . this is not advice ) . my only real complaint is that it can transfer onto the nose pads of my glasses when it is hot , although that is probably partly a glasses-fit problem caused by my low nose bridge . it is also completely irrelevant whenever i wear contacts , so i can live with it .

elsewhere in beauty developments , i am attempting to stop getting lash extensions . i love how they look , which is unfortunately exactly how i became dependent on them (╥﹏╥) the grow-out is annoying , individual extensions can flip around or poke me in the eye , and the maintenance and possible lash-health consequences are not especially appealing . people getting concerned because i'm wincing from my eye tearing up due to them is embarrassing (T-T) for now i am trying to embrace my natural lashes and eventually figure out what combination of mascara and curling will make me happy enough not to miss the extensions . i do not think i will ever truly become a falsie girl . .

i have at least found a much easier lip routine : a tiny amount of lipstick on both lips ( but mostly the bottom one ) and then smudging it into place . it looks softer and cuter on me than trying to define everything perfectly with liner , and i can use my existing liners the same way by treating them as colour to blend instead of a mandatory outline (˶ˆ꒳ˆ˵)
