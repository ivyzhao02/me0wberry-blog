international / treaty-based protection

the Berne Convention provides automatic protection + national treatment for qualifying works in its member countries . that generally means eligible foreign works receive the protection a country gives its own nationals , rather than needing a copyright purchase in every country .

there is no single worldwide copyright registration or enforcement office . protection , exceptions , procedures + remedies depend on the relevant country's law . neither CIPO nor this notice promises to enforce rights internationally .
